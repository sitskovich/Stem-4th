# Lesson 23: Bundle 2 Review - "The Earth Science Games"

**Grade:** 4
**Duration:** 45 Minutes
**Focus:** Review of Lessons 13-22 (Weathering, Erosion, Plate Tectonics, Natural Hazards, Resources)

## Overview
This lesson is a high-energy review session designed to prepare students for the Bundle 2 Earth Science Test. Instead of a traditional lecture, students will rotate through interactive games that reinforce vocabulary, core concepts, and the "Big Picture" mining mission.

## Objectives
- Review key vocabulary: Weathering, Erosion, Deposition, Tectonic Plates, Mitigation, Renewable/Nonrenewable Resources.
- Recall patterns of Earth's features (Ring of Fire, earthquakes, volcanoes).
- Connect scientific concepts to the Mining Mission (safety and resource management).

---

## 1. Warm-Up: "Pictionary" Relay (5 Minutes)
**Goal:** Quick visual recall of key terms.
- **Setup:** Divide class into teams. One student from each team comes to the board.
- **Action:** Teacher whispers a word (e.g., "Volcano", "Delta", "Fossil"). Students draw it. First team to guess correctly gets a point.
- **Terms:** Canyon, Earthquake, Wind Turbine, Coal, Flood.

---

## 2. Main Event: Game Stations (30 Minutes)
*Choose one of the following formats based on class energy/setup:*
*Option A: Rotations (Groups move every 7-8 mins)*
*Option B: Whole Class (Play one game for 15 mins, then another)*

### Game 1: "Memory Match" (Vocabulary Flip Cards)
**Materials:** Sets of cards (Term on one, Definition/Image on the other).
**How to Play:**
1.  Cards are laid face down in a grid (e.g., 4x4).
2.  Students take turns flipping two cards.
3.  If they match (e.g., "Erosion" and "Movement of sediment"), they keep the pair.
4.  **Key Pairs:**
    -   *Weathering* <-> *Breaking down rocks*
    -   *Erosion* <-> *Moving rocks/soil*
    -   *Deposition* <-> *Dropping sediment*
    -   *Tectonic Plate* <-> *Large slab of Earth's crust*
    -   *Ring of Fire* <-> *Zone of many earthquakes/volcanoes*
    -   *Mitigation* <-> *Reducing damage from hazards*

### Game 2: "Four Corners" (Main Ideas)
**Materials:** Signs labeled A, B, C, D in corners of the room.
**How to Play:**
1.  Teacher reads a multiple-choice question (similar to the test).
2.  Students have 10 seconds to move to the corner they think is correct.
3.  Teacher reveals answer. Students in the wrong corner sitting out (or just get a strike).
**Sample Questions:**
-   *Q: What causes a canyon to form?* (A: Wind, B: Ice, C: River Erosion, D: Volcano). **Answer: C**
-   *Q: Where are most earthquakes found?* (A: Middle of ocean, B: Plate Boundaries, C: Deserts, D: Grasslands). **Answer: B**
-   *Q: Which is a renewable resource?* (A: Coal, B: Solar Energy, C: Natural Gas, D: Oil). **Answer: B**

### Game 3: "Earth Science Charades/Hot Potato" (Process Review)
**Materials:** "Hot Potato" (a ball or soft object).
**How to Play (Hot Potato):**
1.  Music plays ("Earthquake Rumble" or energetic song). Students pass the potato.
2.  Music stops. Student holding the potato must answer a question or act out a process.
**Sample Acts:**
-   Act out a *Tsunami* (Wave getting bigger).
-   Act out *Sedimentation* (Layering up).
-   Act out a *Landslide* (Fast movement down a slope).

### Game 4: "Mission Jeopardy" (Team Challenge)
**Materials:** Projector or Whiteboard with columns.
**Categories:**
1.  **Changing Land** (Weathering/Erosion questions)
2.  **Shaky Ground** (Plate Tectonics/Quakes)
3.  **Power Up** (Resources)
4.  **Danger Zone** (Hazards & Mitigation)

---

## 3. Mission Debrief: The Mining Connection (10 Minutes)
**Goal:** Tie everything back to the Coal Mining Recruitment Ad (Mission).
- **Discussion:** "We've reviewed the science. Now, how does this help us with our Mining Mission?"
- **Key Points to Cover:**
    1.  **Location Safety:** "Why do we care about the Ring of Fire?" (Our mine needs to be safe from earthquakes! We need a mitigation plan.)
    2.  **Resource Responsibility:** "We are mining coal (Nonrenewable). How can we show we care about the planet?" (We can propose using some Renewable energy for the mine office, like solar panels.)
    3.  **Erosion Control:** "Mining digs up Earth. What happens if it rains?" (Erosion! We need to plant trees/grass to hold soil in place.)

---

## 4. Assessment Prep
- Remind students that **Lesson 24 is the TEST**.
- **Tip:** "Remember the difference between Weathering (Breaking) and Erosion (Moving)!"
- **Homework (Optional):** Review Vocabulary Cards.

## Teacher Notes
- **Assessment:** Informal assessment during games. Watch for students struggling with specific concepts (e.g., confusing renewable/nonrenewable) and provide on-the-spot correction.
- **Differentiation:** Pair students for Memory Match so stronger readers can help. Use pictures for Charades cards.
