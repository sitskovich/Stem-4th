Bundle 1: Organism Structures & Behavior

Lesson 1: Introduction to Organism Structures
Learn about basic structures of plants and animals and how they help organisms survive.
Lesson 2: Plant Structures and Functions
Explore plant parts, photosynthesis, and root systems with interactive activities.
Lesson 3: Animal Structures and Adaptations
Compare animal structures across species and understand adaptation mechanisms.
Lesson 4: Plant Structure Investigation Lab
Hands-on plant dissection with microscope examination and scientific drawing.
Lesson 5: Food Chains and Energy Flow
Understand food chain relationships and energy flow through ecosystems.
Lesson 6: Ecosystem Interactions
Explore predator-prey relationships and ecosystem balance.
Lesson 7: Human Impact on Ecosystems
Conservation, pollution, and sustainability practices.
Lesson 8: Ecosystem Survey Lab
Field survey, species identification, data collection, and analysis.
Lesson 9: Adaptation and Evolution
Natural selection and adaptation case studies.
Lesson 10: Biodiversity and Classification
Taxonomy and ecosystem diversity.
Lesson 11: Life Cycles and Reproduction
Metamorphosis and reproduction strategies.
Lesson 12: Design a Creature Lab
45-min partner lab: Design creature with 5 adaptations, build 3D model, explain survival.

Bundle 2: Earth's Surface & Resources

Lesson 13: Introduction to Earth's Surface
Explore mountains, valleys, plains, and other landforms that shape our planet.
Lesson 14: Rock Patterns Deep Dive
Analyze fossil evidence and rock layers to understand Earth's changing surface over time.
Lesson 15: Changing Land: Weathering & Erosion
Understand how wind, water, and ice change Earth's surface over time.
Lesson 16: Changing Land Investigation Lab
Hands-on experiments testing waves, wind, and slope effects on erosion.
Lesson 17: Plate Tectonics: Patterns of Earth's Features
Analyze maps to discover patterns of earthquakes, volcanoes, and mountain ranges.
Lesson 18: Plate Tectonics & Earth's Interior
Connect mantle convection to plate movement and Earth's changing surface.
Lesson 19: Renewable vs. Nonrenewable Resources
Classify energy sources and explore environmental impacts of resource use.
Lesson 20: Conservation & Mission Planning
Develop strategies to reduce resource use and plan the mining recruitment ad.
Lesson 21: Natural Processes: Hazard Identification
Study earthquakes, floods, tsunamis, and volcanic eruptions and their impacts.
Lesson 22: Engineering Challenge: Resilient Structures
Design and test structures that can withstand earthquakes, floods, and wind.
Lesson 23: Bundle 2 Review: "The Earth Science Games"
Review games for vocabulary and concepts: Memory Match, Four Corners, Jeopardy, and Mission Debrief.
Lesson 24: Earth Science Test
Final assessment covering Lessons 13-22 (Weathering, Erosion, Plate Tectonics, Resources, Hazards).

Bundle 3: Energy & Forces

Lesson 25: Energy Basics & PE/KE
Introduction to different forms of energy and the relationship between potential and kinetic energy.
Lesson 26: Conservation & Transformation
Investigate how energy changes forms and the Law of Conservation of Energy.
Lesson 27: Energy of Motion
Explore how mass and speed affect the kinetic energy of an object.
Lesson 28: LAB 1: Collision Chaos
Hands-on investigation of energy transfer during collisions using marbles and ramps.
Lesson 29: Mechanical & Sound Waves
Explore how sound travels through waves and vibrations in different mediums.
Lesson 30: Thermal Dynamics
Investigate heat transfer through conduction, convection, and radiation.
Lesson 31: Light & Optics
Explore the properties of light, including reflection, refraction, and the visible spectrum.
Lesson 32: LAB 2: Insulation Investigation
Test different materials to identify effective thermal insulators and conductors.
Lesson 33: Electrical Systems
Build and test circuits to understand series and parallel electrical flow.
Lesson 34: Electromagnetism
Discover the link between electricity and magnetism through hands-on modeling.
Lesson 35: Sustainability & Future Energy
Study renewable energy sources and sustainable power grid engineering.
Lesson 36: LAB 3: The Transformer Challenge
Engineering challenge: Build a system that transforms energy across multiple stages.
Lesson 37: Bundle 3 Review
Interactive review games and vocabulary reinforcement for Bundle 3 concepts.
Lesson 38: Bundle 3 Test
Final assessment covering Energy, Waves, Light, Heat, and Circuits.

Bundle 4: Communicating Using Wave Energy

Lesson 39: What is a Wave?
Discover how waves carry energy through matter and explore wave phenomena all around us.
Lesson 40: Amplitude & Wavelength
Describe waves using amplitude and wavelength and model how wave properties affect energy.
Lesson 41: LAB 1 — Wave Properties Lab
Hands-on investigation using tone generators and slinkies to observe wave properties in action.
Lesson 42: Sound Waves
Investigate how sound travels through vibrations in a medium and how we perceive it.
Lesson 43: Light Waves
Explore how light travels in straight lines and behaves when it hits different materials.
Lesson 44: Reflection & Refraction
Model how light bends and bounces and why objects look different through different materials.
Lesson 45: LAB 2 — Light & Reflection Lab
Hands-on investigation using mirrors and solar cells to explore how light transfers energy.
Lesson 46: Patterns & Information Transfer
Discover how patterns in light and sound carry information from one place to another.
Lesson 47: Morse Code & Signals
Decode and encode messages using Morse code and explore how the telegraph changed communication.
Lesson 48: Binary & Digital Information
Learn how computers use patterns of 0s and 1s to store and transfer information using waves.
Lesson 49: LAB 3 — Design a Communication Device
Engineering challenge: Design and build a device that uses wave patterns to transfer a message.
Lesson 50: Lenses, Cameras & Digital Devices
Explore how lenses and digital devices use waves to extend human senses and capture information.
Lesson 51: Communication Tech: Then & Now
Compare historical and modern communication technologies and how wave science drives innovation.
Lesson 52: Bundle 4 Review — The Wave Games
Station rotations and mini-challenges to review Bundle 4 concepts.
Lesson 53: Bundle 4 Assessment
Summative assessment covering waves, light, and information transfer.
Lesson 54: Bundle 4 Assessment
Final assessment covering Communicating Using Wave Energy — 4-PS4-1, 4-PS4-2, 4-PS4-3.
