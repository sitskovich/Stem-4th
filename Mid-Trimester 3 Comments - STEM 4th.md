# Mid-Trimester 3 STEM Comments
**Curriculum Focus: Bundle 4 - Wave Energy & Communication (Lessons 39-44)**

---

### Mid-Trimester Update (All Students)
*This is a general curriculum update comment to share with all families.*

> "In our STEM class this trimester, we have been exploring the fascinating world of Wave Energy and Communication. Students have learned that waves are disturbances that transfer energy — not matter — through different materials, and have investigated key wave properties such as amplitude and wavelength. We dove into the science of sound, discovering how vibrations travel through solids, liquids, and gases at different speeds. Most recently, students examined how light waves travel in straight lines, interact with the electromagnetic spectrum, and change direction through reflection and refraction. It has been an exciting unit full of hands-on investigations and real-world connections!"
