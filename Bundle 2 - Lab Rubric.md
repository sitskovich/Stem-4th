BUNDLE 2 LAB RUBRIC
Grades 4 – Earth Science Labs (Lessons 16 & 22)

Use this common rubric for all Bundle 2 lab experiences. Score each category using the 4-point scale provided; multiply the point value if you need totals that match your grading system.

| Category | Points | Exceeds Expectations (4) | Meets Expectations (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) |
|----------|--------|---------------------------|-------------------------|----------------|---------------|
| Procedure & Safety | 10 | Follows every step independently; models exemplary safety and station management; proactively supports classmates. | Completes steps with minimal reminders; uses safety gear and handles materials responsibly. | Misses or rushes steps and needs frequent reminders about safety or cleanup. | Disregards instructions; unsafe behavior or refusal to participate. |
| Data & Documentation | 10 | Records detailed measurements/observations; includes labeled sketches/tables; connects data to variables clearly. | Completes the required tables/notes with accurate observations and basic sketches. | Data entries are incomplete or contain errors; sketches lack labels or clarity. | Little to no data recorded; work is illegible or lost. |
| Analysis & Reasoning | 8 | CER or reflection includes precise claims, multiple evidence points, and strong scientific reasoning referencing lab variables. | Provides clear claim with relevant evidence and explanation that links to the investigation. | Explanation is partial or vague; limited evidence cited; reasoning loosely connected. | No meaningful explanation; misconceptions go uncorrected. |
| Collaboration, Roles & Cleanup | 4 | Team collaborates smoothly, fulfills roles, and leaves workstation cleaner than found. | Shares responsibilities, completes cleanup checklist, communicates respectfully. | Uneven participation or incomplete cleanup; occasional off-task behavior. | Disruptive, unsafe, or fails to assist with cleanup/roles. |

TOTAL POSSIBLE: 32 POINTS (adjust weighting to fit lesson needs).

NOTES FOR TEACHERS
- Attach this rubric to each lab recording sheet so students know expectations.
- Highlight the categories you will emphasize for a specific lab (e.g., Lesson 16: focus on Data & Analysis; Lesson 22: focus on Design Iteration).
- For quick scoring, convert to a 4-point overall scale or double the points to reach 64 as needed.
