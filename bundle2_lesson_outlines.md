## Lesson 13 – Rock Patterns: Evidence of Earth’s Past (Scope 1)
- Warm-up: “What can rock layers tell us?” – prompt students to observe Grand Canyon photo **[image placeholder: stratified canyon wall with labels]** `bundle2_guide.txt L231-L244`.
- Objectives & Vocabulary: introduce **rock layer, fossil, sediment, earthquake, relative age** `vocabulary-cards.html bundle2 entries` and align with 4-ESS1-1 focus `bundle2_guide.txt L115-L188`.
- Concept Slide 1: Explain how scientists read rock layers to infer past environments; include diagram of layered strata with fossil types **[image placeholder: cross-section with fossil icons]`bundle2_guide.txt L142-L188`**.
- Concept Slide 2: Model earthquake effects on layers (clay model) referencing Explore 2 `bundle2_guide.txt L204-L247`.
- Guided Practice: Students compare two layer diagrams, identify events (faulting, deposition) and respond to prompt.
- STEM Connections: Introduce STEMscopedia/Linking Literacy summary bullet list `bundle2_guide.txt L249-L278`.
- Application: CER mini-task – interpret provided rock layer sketch (adapt from Evaluate prompt) `bundle2_guide.txt L289-L303`.
- Digital Citizenship: Cyber Pop-Out aligning with research (Website credibility) to vet geological sources.
- Exit Ticket: “Describe one piece of evidence that shows an environment changed and what caused it.”
