# Bundle 3: Using Energy Transformations - Educator's Curriculum Plan

As a STEM educator with 30+ years of experience, I’ve structured this bundle to move from the **concrete** (collisions you can see) to the **abstract** (electricity and thermal flow), culminating in a **design challenge** that applies all concepts.

## 12-Lesson Flow

### Phase 1: Mechanics & Collision (The "Smash" Phase)
- **Lesson 25: Energy Basics & The "Why"**
    - Warm-up: 3D interactive "Energy Ball" that changes color based on height (Potential) and speed (Kinetic).
    - Concept: Defining energy as the ability to do work.
    - **[3JS Visual Concept: A draggable 3D pendulum showing energy swapping between PE and KE]**
- **Lesson 26: The Speed-Energy Connection**
    - Concept: How faster objects carry more energy (4-PS3-1).
    - Guided Practice: Predict "Splash" sizes of marbles dropped from different heights.
- **Lesson 27: LAB 1 - Collision Chaos**
    - Activity: Hands-on collision lab using toy cars and stationary "targets."
    - Focus: Observing energy transfer from a moving object to a stationary one (4-PS3-3).

### Phase 2: Waves & Invisible Transfer (The "Flow" Phase)
- **Lesson 28: Sound: Energy on the Move**
    - Concept: Vibrations and the movement of energy through air/water.
    - **[3JS Visual Concept: A 3D sound wave simulator where students change frequency/amplitude]**
- **Lesson 29: Heat: The Thermal Dance**
    - Concept: Conduction, Convection, and Radiation.
    - Real-world: Why a metal spoon feels colder than a wooden one in the same room.
- **Lesson 30: LAB 2 - Insulation Investigation**
    - Activity: Testing which materials keep a "cup of cocoa" (warm water) hot the longest.
    - Focus: Measuring temperature change over time using digital or analog thermometers.

### Phase 3: Electricity & Light (The "Power" Phase)
- **Lesson 31: Light: Speed & Reflection**
    - Concept: Light as energy that travels in straight lines until it hits something.
    - Interaction: Virtual "Mirror Maze" to predict path of travel.
- **Lesson 32: Electrical Circuits 101**
    - Concept: The flow of electrons in a closed loop (4-PS3-2).
    - **[3JS Visual Concept: A 3D "Inner Wire" view where electrons (glow spheres) move faster when voltage increases]**
- **Lesson 33: LAB 3 - The Energy Transformer Challenge**
    - Activity: Engineering task—Build a device that converts light or electrical energy into motion (e.g., solar car or battery-powered fan).
    - Focus: Constructing a device that converts energy from one form to another (4-PS3-4).

### Phase 4: Big Picture & Mastery
- **Lesson 34: Energy for the Future**
    - Concept: Renewable vs. Non-renewable. Mapping energy sources.
    - Debate: The pros/cons of Wind, Solar, and Nuclear.
- **Lesson 35: Bundle 3 Review: The Energy Games**
    - Review: Gamified stations covering the 3 Labs and core vocabulary.
- **Lesson 36: Final Board: Bundle 3 Assessment**
    - Test: Comprehensive check on Energy Transformations.

## 3JS Integration Strategy
To truly "wow" the students, we will implement **The Energy Lab Hub**:
1. **Collision Simulator**: Let students choose mass and velocity in 3D to see real-time impact energy.
2. **Circuit Surgeon**: A sandbox to drag/drop batteries, wires, and bulbs in 3D space to see why "short circuits" happen.
3. **Thermal Vision**: A 3D model of a house where students drag different insulation materials to see "heat leak" visuals.
